XAI-Guided Ensemble Selection for Healthcare Data

I developed an Explainable AI-guided ensemble selection framework for highly imbalanced healthcare datasets. The approach integrates SHAP explanations, AUPRC-based performance evaluation, explanation stability, and model diversity into the ensemble selection process.

I used nested cross-validation throughout to reduce data leakage risk and improve the reliability of the results.
